Karyotypes, mosaics and pedigrees
from a genotyping array

arraykaryo reads a cohort's array export once and writes what a cytogeneticist would want to know about every sample: its sex-chromosome complement, who it is related to, and every near-chromosomal gain, loss, copy-neutral LOH or run of homozygosity, with the share of cells that carries it.

Everything on this page comes from one run of arraykaryo run on a public 1000 Genomes Project cohort: 2,141 samples, 2,421,634 probes, T2T-CHM13v2.0 coordinates. Nothing was curated by hand; the numbers and drawings are read from the run's own tables.

The whole cohort at once

Every event, on the chromosomes

Each row is a chromosome of T2T-CHM13v2.0 drawn to scale, bands shaded as a karyogram. Above it, every gain (orange), loss (blue) and copy-neutral LOH (pink) called in the cohort that survives the artefact map; below it, the runs of homozygosity of 10 Mb or more (gold). 999 events on the picture, the longest nearest the chromosome; hover any one for the sample and its cell fraction. The X's and Y's lost or gained in part of the cells, the sex step's findings, are on the sex plot below rather than here. Whole chromosomes stand out as bars across the row: a whole chromosome without heterozygosity of chromosome 21 in NA20509 (every cell); a gain of chromosome 12 in HG02322 (84% of cells); a gain of chromosome 9 in NA19208 (72% of cells); a gain of chromosome 12 in HG01357 (66% of cells); a gain of chromosome 12 in NA20759 (49% of cells); a gain of chromosome 9 in NA21143 (14% of cells); copy-neutral LOH of chromosome 21 in HG01461 (14% of cells).

The run called 7,094 events on 1,795 samples in all. Most are not on the picture: 4,539 of the 5,343 autosomal calls carry a tag - recurrent (the same reading in 2% or more of the cohort over the same megabase: a probe cluster the array reads oddly, or a common copy-number polymorphism), probe-sparse, or inside a listed artefact region (the MHC, the 8p23.1 and 17q21.31 inversions, centromere flanks, chromosome ends) - and almost all of those are read from the LRR alone, with no heterozygous BAF to split. They stay in the event table with their tags and off the digest. Of the 804 that remain, 428 have the BAF split behind them, 23 are copy changes in every cell read from the LRR alone, and 353 are runs of homozygosity of 10 Mb or more.

Checked against sequencing

The same mosaics, read from the array

Two of these cell lines were read earlier from whole-genome sequencing by triokaryo. The array finds the same events at the same places. The cell fractions differ because the array's DNA and the sequenced DNA are different cultures of a lymphoblastoid line, and a clone's share changes with passage.

What a culture does to a cell line

Whole chromosomes gained, and a chromosome without heterozygosity

Lymphoblastoid lines acquire changes in culture, and the 1000 Genomes lines are no exception: 7 whole autosomes are changed in this cohort. arraykaryo lists them as findings with their cell fraction rather than treating them as errors; a digest for hand-off opens with them.

Sex chromosomes

Every sample's X against its Y

The X level of a sample (its median corrected LRR outside the pseudoautosomal regions) against its Y level (over the probes that are Y-specific in this cohort), from the run's sex.tsv. The cohort's own modes set the scale: one X at -0.40, two at 0.01; the Y present at 0.01 and absent at -3.92. Everything between the modes is a mosaic, confirmed on the X by the BAF split at heterozygous calls.

Complements: 46,XX 1088, 46,XY 1045, 47,XXY 2, 45,X 6. In culture, 10 men lost the Y from 23-85% of their cells, 23 women lost an X from 13-64% and 6 gained one in 13-45%. Against the reported sex, 2,126 of 2,130 agree. The ones that do not: HG01465 is 46,XX in a reported man; HG01683 is 47,XXY in a reported woman; HG01896 is 46,XX in a reported man; HG02372 is 47,XXY in a reported man.

Relatedness

Who is related to whom, from the genotypes alone

875 related pairs: 719 parent-offspring, 26 full-sibling, 64 second-degree, 66 third-degree. From them 331 trios and 47 duos, each trio oriented by its Mendelian-error rate against the cohort's own floor.

Against the published 1000 Genomes pedigree, 779 claimed relationships agree and no claimed parent is unrelated. The genotypes also find 29 related pairs the table does not list, the cryptic relatedness of the collection, and one pair listed as third order that is in fact full siblings.

The trios the brief named are recovered: NA12878 with NA12891 and NA12892, NA19240 with NA19239 and NA19238, HG00733 with HG00731 and HG00732, HG01103 with HG01101 and HG01102. The "HG00702 trio" is a three-generation family, which is what the table says once read closely and what the genotypes find.

What it can and cannot see

Calibrated, and honest about the floor

Written down, not hidden. This platform compresses one copy to an LRR of -0.40 rather than the textbook -0.55, so the cohort's own scale is used and fractions read from the LRR alone run low. The baseline BAF split of a sample sets what the BAF can confirm. Centromere flanks, the 9p11-q21 heterochromatin of CHM13 and a few common copy-number polymorphisms produce the commonest raw readings; the cohort's own artefact map tags them.

A low-level copy-neutral LOH pattern on 4q, at 8-14% of cells in 45 lines, sits at the floor of the LOH rule in BAF-noisy samples and is treated as the platform's until one is confirmed. The 0.06 BAF-SD threshold flags 12% of this cohort, which is read with the flag beside every call.

Every event is a reading of LRR, BAF and genotypes, to be confirmed by a clinical method before it means anything about a person. These are research cell lines.

Run it

One command on your own export

An Illumina array cohort exported to VCF/BCF with FORMAT GT, LRR and BAF (as bcftools +gtc2vcf writes), on GRCh37, GRCh38 or T2T-CHM13. A sample sheet with the reported sex and a pedigree are optional and are checked, never trusted over the genotypes.

pip install git+https://github.com/jlanej/arraykaryo
arraykaryo run --bcf cohort.bcf --out out --threads 8 --sample-sheet samples.tsv --pedigree trios.tsv
#   a cohort of thousands: add --pages digest;  an export without INFO/GC: add --fasta ref.fa

# or the container, which carries bcftools and everything else
docker run --rm -v "$PWD:/work" ghcr.io/jlanej/arraykaryo:main arraykaryo run --bcf /work/cohort.bcf --out /work/out --threads 8

Out come qc/, sex/, pedigree/, events/ (every event with its evidence, every run of homozygosity, a karyotype string per sample, figures) and report/: a self-contained HTML report with per-sample pages, a guide to every column and rule, and the digest. The methods, with their thresholds and sources, are in METHODS.md; every output column in OUTPUT.md; the example's full numbers in the example README.

arraykaryo 1.1.0, MIT. The cohort is the 1000 Genomes Project's lymphoblastoid cell lines genotyped on an Illumina array and exported with bcftools +gtc2vcf on T2T-CHM13; the reported sexes and the known pedigree are the IGSR's public tables. All of it is public data, and nothing here is a clinical finding about a person.

Built by scripts/build_site.py from the run's tables: the karyogram and the sex plot are drawn from events.all.tsv and sex.tsv as SVG, the dense figures are the run's own PNGs.
